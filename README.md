## Projects

### Substrate
An AI governance and decision-control layer that enables businesses to define, customise, and enforce their own rules for AI systems. Substrate evaluates AI actions against company-specific policies, supports human oversight and approval, and maintains transparent audit trails, giving organisations greater control, accountability, and flexibility over how their AI operates.

https://substratecontrol.org/

### Curio
An educational video discovery platform focused on
meaningful learning rather than doomscrolling and useless content, paired with learning games, social features, and a nostalgic frutiger aero style to act as a call back to a time of the internet before slop and useless content was the norm.

### Perspective
A visual planning and goal-tracking application built
around an interactive node canvas.

### Arc AI
An experimental AI chat interface exploring model
selection, conversations and file-based interaction.
